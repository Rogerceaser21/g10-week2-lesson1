what we settled last lesson

A vague prompt, and a blueprint

Make me a Tetris game.

That leaves the artificial intelligence guessing everything. A detailed prompt hands it a blueprint instead. Rubbish in, rubbish out.

Gemini is a large language model. It learns patterns from a huge amount of text and code, then predicts the next piece. Not thinking, not searching, which is why it can be confidently wrong.

Your Product Requirements Document, or PRD, is that blueprint. Seven headings:

GoalPlayersScreenRulesControlsScoringDone-when

Done-when is the line a tester can check. It is the one you check your own game against after the changeover.

brain break · end of lap 1

Boi-ing 7

  • Students stand in a circle facing inwards.
  • A nominated student begins by saying "one".
  • The student to the left of the one says "two" and so on around the circle, calling out numbers in numerical order.
  • Every time a number comes along that contains a "7" or is a multiple of 7, students must call out "boi-ing" (a jumping sound because "7" is being jumped) in place of the actual number. Counting then continues as normal until the next "7".

RACE: four things a change prompt carries

ROLEACTIONCONTEXTEXPECTATION

Role

Who the artificial intelligence acts as.

Action

What to do.

Context

What it needs to know about your game.

Expectation

What the result should look like or do.

Four lines, no more. Two worked examples next, then you write your own.

worked example 1 · change the colours

A RACE prompt that changes one thing

You are a game developer. Change all the Tetris block colours to different shades of electric blue. This is a React-based Tetris game running in Canvas mode. Each block type should still be a distinct shade so players can tell them apart.

Role

game developer

Action

the colours

Context

React Tetris in Canvas

Expectation

distinct shades

worked example 2 · add a feature

A RACE prompt that adds something new

You are a game developer. Add a bomb block that appears every time 5 rows are cleared. This is a React-based Tetris game with score tracking. The bomb should be a gold colour, clear a 3 by 3 area when it lands, and trigger a short explosion animation with a boom sound.

Structure beats length. Four clear lines beat a long vague paragraph.

Notice the Expectation line does the heavy lifting: gold, a 3 by 3 area, an animation, a sound.

brain break · end of lap 2

Heads Down, Thumbs Up

  • All players sit with their heads down on their arms and eyes closed. One thumb is raised.
  • The leader chooses 2 to 4 Tappers (those at the front know who they are; others do not).
  • Tappers walk quietly around the room and each tap one player's thumb gently.
  • Tappers return to the front of the room.
  • The leader gives the signal: everyone lifts their heads and opens their eyes.
  • Each tapped player stands. One at a time, they guess which Tapper touched them.
Correct guess: the Guesser and Tapper swap roles. Incorrect guess: the Tapper stays in their role for the next round.
